Review

Labor Code 6401.9(d)(1)(B) says the log "shall be reviewed during the periodic reviews of the plan", and (c)(2)(L) says the plan is reviewed at least annually, when a deficiency becomes apparent, and after every workplace violence incident. So reading this book is itself a duty, and this page is the reading.
