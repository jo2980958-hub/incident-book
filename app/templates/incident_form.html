Say what happened, and let the software write the first version

Fields (C) and (H) are the two that have to be written in sentences, and the person who has to write them has just had something happen to them. Type or dictate a few words here and Amazon Bedrock drafts a first version of both. It is a draft and nothing else. It goes into the boxes below for you to change, and it only becomes part of the log when you press save with your name against it. Every classification on this page stays yours: the software is not asked who did it, and it is never told to guess.
