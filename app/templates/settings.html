Settings

Three decisions, and nothing else. Who keeps this book, what each camera can and cannot see, and whether the software may draft wording for the two free-text fields.

The premises

Every document this app produces carries the name and address of the business at the top, because a police officer or an insurer handed a pack with no letterhead cannot tell which shop it came from. An install can hold more than one: an owner with a shop and a unit round the corner keeps one book, not two.

What each camera can see

Ring tells us where a camera is registered and nothing about what it cannot see. A record that never says where the blind spots are invites a reader to treat an absence as evidence. What you write here prints in the provenance block of every document from that camera.

Drafting

The software may draft fields (C) and (H) from a few words typed by whoever was there. It never chooses a classification and it never files anything: a person edits and saves, and the record says which of those two happened. Turn it off entirely by starting the app with INCIDENT_BOOK_BEDROCK=off, and everything else works unchanged.
